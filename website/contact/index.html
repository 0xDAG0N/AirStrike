Contact Airstrike

Need to reach the Airstrike maintainers? Use the channels below so your request lands with the right person.

Support

Technical Support

Check the documentation and FAQ first. If you are still blocked:

  • Open an issue on GitHub with logs, adapter details, and reproduction steps.
  • Email us at root@0xdagon.me for questions that include sensitive data.
Community Help

Join the conversation through the links on our team and social pages to collaborate with other operators.

Bug Reports & Features

Found a defect or have an idea to improve the UI or attacks? Submit it on GitHub Issues. Include logs generated by the Results tab and the adapter you tested with.

Security & Vulnerabilities

If you discover a vulnerability in Airstrike or its infrastructure:

  • Email root@0xdagon.me with the subject line [Security].
  • Provide a clear description, reproduction steps, and any proof-of-concept artifacts.
  • We will acknowledge receipt within 72 hours and coordinate disclosure if required.

Press & Collaboration

For interviews, partnerships, or demo requests, email root@0xdagon.me. Please include your organization, timeline, and the nature of your inquiry.

Legal & Trademark

Questions about licensing, attribution, or trademark usage can be sent to root@0xdagon.me. We’ll guide you through proper crediting and usage guidelines.

Airstrike is a community-driven project; respectful, detailed communication helps us respond faster. Thank you for helping keep the platform stable and secure.
